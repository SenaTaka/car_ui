Unit support: gauges now read in mph, °F, psi and mpg based on your region (you can also switch manually in Settings). CSV exports follow the same units, with the unit written into the column header.

• Vehicle profile: set your tachometer range, redline and fuel type (gasoline/diesel). Fuel economy is now estimated correctly for diesels.
• When an adapter will not connect, the connection screen now explains why and what to try, plus guidance on choosing an adapter.
• Start and stop recording, and see recording status, from any tab.
• Gauges that have stopped updating are now clearly marked.
• The screen stays awake while the HUD is showing, and brightness is raised automatically.
• Settings have been reorganised into standard sections, with technical details and the communication log moved one level down.
• Fixed decimal separators for German, French and Spanish.
• Fixed layout problems at large text sizes.
